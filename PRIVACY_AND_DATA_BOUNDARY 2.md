# Privacy and data boundary

This is a public profile repository. It should contain only information and
assets intentionally approved for public display. It is not a personal-data
directory, identity-verification source, recruitment system, or license to
scrape or republish linked people's information.

Do not add private contact details, government or clinical identifiers,
credentials, unpublished work, confidential employer material, private
messages, third-party personal data, or assets without permission. Linked
projects, institutions, logos, fonts, photographs, and trademarks remain under
their owners' terms.

The repository licenses are split by material: MIT for code and CC BY 4.0 for
original profile content. Neither license permits impersonation, endorsement
claims, or reuse of third-party materials. A hosted portfolio operator must
publish its own privacy notice and terms if it introduces analytics, contact
forms, cookies, mailing lists, or other collection.
